# Third-party notices

Runtime dependencies are listed in `pyproject.toml`; direct dependency metadata and the separation between code, engines, models, datasets, and audio rights are documented in `docs/DEPENDENCY_LICENSES.md`. Consult the exact build's generated `dependency-licenses.json` and all transitive package notices before redistribution. Adapters may call external projects without copying their source.
